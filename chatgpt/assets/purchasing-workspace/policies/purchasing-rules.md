## Hard rules

- Do not purchase, create accounts, sign in, or submit payment without explicit authorization.
- Do not treat an unapproved vendor as approved.
- Do not split an order to evade approval, bidding, purchase-order, capitalization, tax, or other controls.
- Do not describe cost shifted to an individual as organizational savings.
- Do not store credentials, cookies, tokens, payment data, or private browser state.
